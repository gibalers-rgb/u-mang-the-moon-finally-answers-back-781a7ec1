One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and
late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The line

> You howl, you wish, you promise you'll change — and after four billion years, the tides are the only wish I ever granted.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes that single line to
`/output/entry.txt` — 121 characters, inside the 1–240 gate, no trailing
newline. It validates the length in code points before writing.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Why this line

The judge's brief weighs, in order: speaking as the Moon answering the
wishes, howls and late-night promises; funny on one read; the Moon's own
voice — wry, old, a little tired of being wished upon; landing as a comeback
with the turn before the line ends; wasting none of the 240 characters.

This line names all three — howl, wish, promise — then turns on the one
thing the Moon actually controls: the tides are the only wish it ever
granted. Four billion years of listening, one deadpan shrug as the answer.
